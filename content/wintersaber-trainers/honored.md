There is an old story under all this, and you have earned it.

Long ago the queen of the sabers was in the grip of rampaging giants, and Tyrande Whisperwind pulled her free. The frostsabers have been friends to my people ever since. The queen is called Shy-Rotam. I think of that rescue every time a cat lets me near her. It was an old kindness, and the cats have never stopped repaying it.

The giants are not gone. The frostmaul are the same ones, and they are still close. I ask you to fight them in her name. To me it is a debt, and you would be helping to pay it. I would not ask it of someone the sabers still hissed at.

I think better of you every time you climb up here.
