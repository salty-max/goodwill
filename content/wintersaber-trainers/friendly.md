Good meat. The sabers ate all of it.

I will ask you for the same thing many times, and I want it done well each time. A young saber learns that way. The lesson comes round again until it stops being a lesson and becomes simply what the cat is.

There is other work now. The Winterfall furbolgs have gone strange. They are frightened of everything, and frightened things kill whatever crosses them. I never raised a hand against them, and I had hoped never to. But they come too near the rock, and I do not want them near the young ones. Push them back. The young ones should not learn fear this early.
