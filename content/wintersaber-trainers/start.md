Hello. Not many climb this far, and those who do usually want a saber.

You can have one in the end, but mine is only half the say. A saber on this rock decides for herself whether she will carry you, and no price changes her mind. You will have to win us both, and the cats are the harder half.

So we start where every young saber starts, with food. They eat a great deal while they train. The chimaera and the bears of these hills carry plenty of meat. Bring me some. It is plain work, and it counts, and it will be the first of many times you do it.
