That is my observatory, up there on the hill. Do not ask what it earns.

A caravan brought me the first lens from Durotar. A ship was meant to bring me the second, and the pirates got the ship. I nearly gave up on the whole thing.

[after: 888] Then you went into their camp and came out with it, and now on a clear night the sky comes right down to Ratchet.

People ask what a goblin wants with the stars. There is no money in them. That is the entire appeal.

Same with Pozzik's racing team. I find parts for them in every port I have, and once I got my own hands black working on one of the rigs, and I have not been that happy on a dock since. No profit in it. None. The gnomes are still closing the gap, and I keep sending parts.

Everything else I own makes money. Those two things just make me glad to get up in the morning. Undermine would want a cut of that too.
