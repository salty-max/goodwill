I have built a lot of things in my life, kid.

A city, once, for an orc warchief who paid on time. You would be surprised how seldom that happens. This port, plank by plank. An observatory nobody asked for. Fuel lines for racers that may never win. I can tell you what went into every one of them, down to the last nail.

The trouble is this. I have the money for one more, and two drawings on the table. A breakwater out past the docks, to keep the Southsea from sailing right up to my warehouses. Or a proper lighthouse on the point, so nobody honest ever runs aground coming in at night. The breakwater is sensible. The lighthouse is beautiful. I have gone back and forth for a month and lost every time.

So I am asking you. You have done more for this Cartel than most goblins who were born into it, and you have no money riding on it either way. Pick one. I will build it, and when anybody asks why that one, I will tell them whose idea it was.

Take your time. Not too much. Lumber is not getting any cheaper.
