Kid! You are starting to turn up in my ledgers on the right side.

Ratchet works like this. Everybody who matters is on the payroll. The wharfmaster, the bruisers, the fellow who checks on the bruisers. Dizzywig keeps the dock logs, and I trust his logs because I pay him.

Then there are the people you cannot pay. Pirates, mostly. And the vice admiral whose whole job is keeping these waters clear, and who could not clear a bathtub. Every time a Freebooter sails past Grezzlik's fleet with my cargo aboard, I write it down. I have a whole book of Grezzlik.

Whatever you have been doing for the Cartel, you have not been doing it for my money. I pay too badly for that. I am still working out what you are doing it for.
