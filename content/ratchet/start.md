Gazlowe. I run Ratchet. Ships come in, ships go out, goods change hands, I take a little off the top, and everybody goes home happy. That is the theory.

The practice is pirates. The Southsea Freebooters hit my ships up and down this coast, and lately they have pitched their tents within a short walk of my docks. The trade princes have sunk a fortune into the fleets that are meant to stop them, and I am still short a lens, a crate of boots and a good night's sleep.
