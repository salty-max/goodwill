The Heedless passed me once at sea, years back. What a hull. Long and low and quick, built, I would swear, by someone who loved boats more than money. I know the sickness. I still have a sketch of her lines somewhere.

Her captain was Baron Longshore, out of Gilneas, and he used that beautiful ship to trouble mine for the Southsea. I put his name on a wanted poster and meant every word of it. I also hoped, a little, that whoever took him down would leave the ship in one piece. You can want a man gone and still want his boat.

That is how it goes with pirates. They steal from you with the best shipwrights' work on the sea, and you hate them for it, and you take notes.

[after: 895] He will not be troubling anybody now. I paid the bounty gladly. I never asked what became of the Heedless. Some things a goblin does not want an invoice for.
