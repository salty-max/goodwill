You've been busy in the hills. The riders talk.

The best gryphon jockey I ever had was a chap called Rhapsody Shindigger. Nobody flew like him. I sent him south once to help a fellow of the Explorers' League, and he came home with a thirst on him that never went away.

We all drink up here, and gladly. Rhapsody stopped being glad about it. I had to take him off the birds before he lost one to the drink, and I haven't forgiven the ale, or myself, or the League, though that last one's not fair. My father dug with them, on the team that found Uldaman. I've a weakness for anyone who goes looking for things.

So when a new rider comes up to the roost, I hold them up against Rhapsody. Not one has measured up yet.
