Thadius Grimshade. Diviner. Lives at Nethergarde now, in the Blasted Lands, among mages and worse. He walked out of the peak a long time back, after knowledge of the darker sort, and plenty up here said good riddance. I never did.

A dwarf can leave the roost and keep the roost in him. Thadius has. I'd bet my beard he keeps a gryphon feather on his desk among all those books. And I'd wager Rhapsody still turns his head when a gryphon flies over Quel'Danil. You don't stop being one of us by flying off, any more than a gryphon stops being a gryphon when the peak is out of reach.

[after: 2993] And when we needed him, Thadius found Sharpbeak for us with one feather and whatever dark arts he keeps at Nethergarde. He asked a price, a troll's medallion, and you paid it. That's Thadius. And he found him all the same.

I've been wondering lately how you'd do on a gryphon. I've a feeling you'd be good. Better than good. It would be a fine thing to find out one day, with the whole roost pretending not to care how it went.
