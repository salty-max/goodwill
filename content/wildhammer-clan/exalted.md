My father spent his whole life digging down. Uldaman, and every hole the League ever asked him to poke his beard into. I spent mine going up. We used to argue about it over the ale, which way the truth was, under the stone or over the clouds. Neither of us won.

He'd have liked you. You go looking, the way he did. You fight for what's ours, the way I'd want. The hills are quieter for you, the trolls thinner, and the riders tell stories about you at the roost, the good kind.

Last week I went out to Quel'Danil to see Rhapsody. First time since I let him go. He was half in a barrel, as usual. I told him about you. He laughed and said it sounded like I'd found another one.

I told him there'd never be another one. Then I told him it had taken me all these years, and you, to stop holding everyone up against him. He didn't say anything to that. He poured me a cup instead, and I drank it with him, and for once it was a good time for one.

Fly well, wherever you go. The trolls are thinner. They aren't gone.
