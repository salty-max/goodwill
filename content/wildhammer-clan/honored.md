Swiftwing's son went missing a few days back. Sharpbeak. The finest young gryphon this roost has hatched in my time, the one we'd all decided would be leading it one day.

He was out hunting with his parents and never came home. Trolls, we knew, but which trolls? The Witherbark cages were searched and turned up nothing. So was the Vilebranch altar, where they do worse than keep things in cages. That was the only good news in a bad week.

To us a gryphon is half the rider and all of the clan's strength. Lose the best of the young ones and the whole roost goes quiet. You could hear it. Nobody sang at supper that night, and up here somebody always sings.

[after: 2994] You brought him home. His mother and father haven't stopped fussing over him since, and the first evening the three of them were back in the roost together, I had to go and stand behind a pillar for a while. The riders were decent enough to look at the sky.
