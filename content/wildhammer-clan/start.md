Mind the roosts, and mind your head. A gryphon that doesn't know you will take it off and think nothing of it.

This is Aerie Peak. We're no part of any Alliance, mind. Our doors are open to it and shut to the Horde, and that's the long and short of it.

Our real trouble is closer to home. The Witherbark have these hills crawling with trolls, and every one of them would sooner have a gryphon on a spit than in the sky. Thin them out, and you'll find the aviary friendlier. The gryphons won't care either way. The riders will.
