The Outriders don't ask who you are any more. I'd say you earned that, but it's a small word for it.

I want to sell something that isn't for killing, one day. Lamps. Good boots for walking, not running. Fishing line. Toys for the clan's young ones, the kind that only make a noise. A cradle or two, carved from wood nobody had to fight for.

I've sold rations and bandages and repairs to the Outriders for so long that I've forgotten what it's like to hand somebody a thing and not wonder whether they'll be alive to use it up.

I won't get my wish soon. I know that. The gulch isn't going anywhere, and neither are the elves, and neither are we.

Every one of you who walks out of that gulch is a little practice for the morning I want.

One day I mean to open this counter on a morning when nobody comes in hurt. I've never had one. Just orcs wanting boots, and a young one after a toy that makes a noise, and nobody waiting on linen. I don't know what I'll say. Something about the boots, probably.
