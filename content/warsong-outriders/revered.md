Every orc in the Outriders can tell you about Grom. Most of them tell it louder than I do, and they leave the middle out.

The Warsong are Hellscream's clan. When the Horde came across the sea, the Warchief sent us here for wood, and Grom took the job, and the forest fought back, and Grom did things to win that the whole Horde paid for. Then he paid for them himself, at the end, with his life, and freed us all doing it. We sing him. We're proud of him. We know the price too.

And the axes haven't stopped. The wood is still wanted. Every log that leaves this valley costs somebody, an elf or an orc, and I know some of the names on our side. The elves know theirs.

I ate my supper on one of their stumps once, and it made a good table. I haven't eaten there since.
