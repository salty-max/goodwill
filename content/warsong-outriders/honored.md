That's the bandages open to you, then. Not that I like selling them as much as the bread.

Every bandage I sell goes on somebody I know. That's the trouble with being the supplier. You hand out the rations at first light, and by dark the same faces come back for linen, and some of them don't come back at all. I know without asking who's due a new pack and who isn't due for anything any more.

The elves aren't easy. They know that forest better than we ever will. They come out of the trees fast and quiet, and they don't give up, not ever, because it's their trees, and I'd fight the same way for mine.

So, bandages. Buy a lot of them, and hand them round. The ones beside you will need them before you do.
