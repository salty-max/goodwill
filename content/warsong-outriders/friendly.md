There you are! The Outriders know your face now. That means rations.

Here. Trail bread, dried meat, a little something for the nerves, all packed for the gulch. Outside the gulch they're no use to anyone, so don't go saving them for a long trip. They're made to be eaten between one fight and the next, standing up, with an elf arrow in the post beside your head.

Half of a war is who ate last, and nobody sings about that half. An orc with something in his belly holds a flag a good deal longer than one without. I can't swing an axe like I used to. I can make sure the ones who do aren't hungry.

Eat. Go fight. The bread will be here.
