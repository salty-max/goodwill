You need not lower your voice. Little that mortals say carries far among my kind. The bronze flight has not favoured one of you in a very long while, and you begin with us where every mortal begins: nowhere at all.

Do not take it hard. We are slow to attend to anything that will not last, and that is all it is.

There, past the walls of the Hold, the hive is moving. The ground around it trembles, and the old enemy readies its war again. That is the thing worth minding. It is why I am here.
