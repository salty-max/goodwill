So. You have begun to matter, a little. I felt it as one feels a single grain slip in an hourglass: hardly at all, and then there is no ignoring it.

What the flight wants of mortals is simple and very hard. Every servant of the insects' masters that falls is a grain moved from their side of the glass to ours. I have tended that glass a long time. It is good to see another hand on it.

A word about the bronze flight, which you have not asked for: we do not hurry. That is not the same as not caring. Remember it, the day we seem slow to you.
