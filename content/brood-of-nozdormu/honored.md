I will give you an old story, if you will take it. I will tell it without ornament.

I was dying once. I had nothing left to give, my wounds were open, and I was no longer afraid, which is how I knew how near the end had come. Then the master of my flight came to me.

I do not remember the breath. I remember that afterwards I was no longer dying, and that I was told to remain, for as long as remaining was needed. So I became a Watcher. I have never found a better word for it than the one I was given.

It was kindly done, and it has cost me ever since. Both of those are true, and I have stopped trying to make one of them smaller than the other.
