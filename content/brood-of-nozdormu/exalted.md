The bronze flight has let its sand run past every mortal who has stood in this desert since the wall went up, and in all that running I had given up on its ever pausing for one. I thought of it the way one thinks of rain here: idly, out of habit, without hope.

It has paused for you, and that means I will stop speaking to you as I speak to a stranger. To me that is a great deal. For a very long time everyone I spoke to was a stranger, even those beside me, as though some part of me were always turned towards the wall.

[after: 8286] You went to the Caverns of Time for me and found the master returned. My watch is over. I do not yet know what a Watcher does when the watching ends. Stay a while, and help me find out.

I have waited a long time for something to arrive, and long ago I stopped picturing what shape it would take. It came with sand in its boots, and it waited with me. Waiting with someone beside me is a different thing, and I had forgotten how different. Whenever you return, however late, I will know you. I am glad to have been wrong about mortals, and the sand will go on falling, a little more gently now.
