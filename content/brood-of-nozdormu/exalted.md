No mortal has held the bronze flight's favor in a thousand years. I used to wonder whether one ever would, the way one wonders about rain in this desert: idly, out of habit, without hope.

You hold it now. It means I will stop speaking to you as I speak to a stranger. That is all it means, and I find it is a great deal. For a very long time everyone I spoke to was a stranger, even those beside me, because some part of me was always turned toward the wall.

[after: 8286] You went to the Caverns of Time for me and found the master returned. My watch is over. I do not yet know what a Watcher does when the watching ends. Stay a while, and help me find out.

I have waited a long time for something to arrive. I did not expect it to be you. Waiting with someone beside me is a different thing, and I had forgotten how different. If you come back, however late, I will know you. I am glad to have been wrong about mortals. Some of them, at least. One.
