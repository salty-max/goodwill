Long ago I swore an oath, and the time to keep it has come at last. What it was is mine. What keeping it feels like, I can give you.

It feels like a door you have watched for so long that you forgot it could open. The hive stirs, the sands shift, the qiraji make ready, and all at once the waiting is over, and I find I did not want it to be. An oath kept is an oath you no longer carry. I have carried this one so long that I do not know my own weight without it.

A Watcher fears one thing, and it is not the enemy. The enemy is honest. What a Watcher fears is the thought that the one you wait for will not come back, and that you will go on waiting anyway, out of habit, long after it means anything.

I have had that thought every day for longer than your kind has had names.
