There's a third side in that valley.

The Winterax trolls. Cannibals, up in the caves, and dwarves would end up in their stew pots, given the chance. Their chief was a brute called Korrak. We killed him. Good. Then the trolls brought him back, the way trolls do. Burn the body, I always say, when a troll falls. Nobody burned this one.

A war has more than two sides, and the ones you forget are the ones that get you. I've lost good soldiers to a spear from the caves while they were busy with the keep. Watch the Frostwolves, and watch the caves. The trolls don't care which flag you carry. Neither does a stew pot.

You've the reverence of the Guard now. There's more rank above this, and I'd like to pin it on a soldier worth it.

[after: 7170] Wear the new insignia into the caves as proudly as onto the field.
