I've given out a great many insignias. The last of them is the Eye of Command, and you're as near to it as a soldier gets.

[after: 7172] There it is. Wear it, and the soldiers on that field will follow what you tell them, for as long as the battle lasts. They'll ask you where to stand, and you'll have to know, every time.

When you first walked up that road, I signed you up the way I sign up everybody, one more name for the list, one more pin on the map. I didn't think twice about it.

I've spent my whole service telling soldiers where to go. You get good at it. You get so good that you stop noticing it's people you're moving about. Then the soldiers start looking to see which way one of the pins went before they pick their own way, and you start noticing again.

So go and give your orders out there, and give them well. I'll be at the mouth of the valley with the spare insignias, and if you've an order for an old recruiter, I'll take it. It's been a long time since anybody gave me one I was glad of.

Go on. They're waiting for you.
