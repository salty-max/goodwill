Name, kin and trade, on the list. The clerk reads it later, or I do.

I sign up soldiers for the Stormpike Guard, and I need a great many of them. The valley eats soldiers the way a forge eats coal. You go in, you fight, you come back out, or you don't, and I sign up the next one. That's the job. I'm good at it.

Every soldier of the Guard wears an insignia, and the insignia grows with the soldier. Show me what you're made of on the field, and I'll see you get a better one. Lose it, and I'll find you another. I always have more.
