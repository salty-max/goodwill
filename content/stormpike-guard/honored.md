Word's gone up the mountain about you. All the way up.

The base talks, and the base writes home, and some of what it writes, between the complaints about the soup, reaches the king himself. Your name did. I've sent a good many soldiers into that valley, and the king hears of very few of them.

He wants that valley. He's made that clear to all of us, in letters with a great deal of seal on them. Every soldier I send in is a piece of what he wants, and most of them, I'm sorry to say, are worn out or gone before he ever learns what they were called.

You come home whole, battle after battle, and you go back in. That's how a name gets up a mountain.

[after: 7169] Take the new insignia. The king knows your name now, so wear it where he'll hear of it.
