You stand on Gelkis earth, and it does not know you.

I hear the earth. It tells me who walks on it, and how heavily, and what they carry in their hearts. You it cannot read yet, so there is no place for you at our fires.

The Magram are our enemies. They are loud, and they believe their strength is enough. The earth is stronger than strength. When the earth knows your name, I will speak with you. Until then, go back the way you came.
