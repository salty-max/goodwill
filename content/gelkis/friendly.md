The earth is heavy with Magram now, and you are the one who made it heavy. This is good. Now we will talk.

Listen. The Gelkis do not fight only with spears. We fight with what the ground gives us. We believe the mother of the centaur, Theradras, lies beneath this land, and that the earth is her voice. We keep her tears in our cave, and while we keep them, she keeps us.

The Magram say nobody protects the centaur. They say it loudly, and they listen to nothing. So they will lose. A clan that listens to nothing has nothing to warn it when the ground shifts.

You are not Gelkis. You never will be. But the earth no longer trembles when you climb to our village, and among my people, that is the first word of friendship.
