The earth has told me a secret about you.

Your people came to Desolace to learn how the centaur are beaten. You chose the Gelkis because we hate the Magram, and hate is a door. When the Magram are weak, your people will turn to the Gelkis, and they will already know how to break us. I knew it from the start. I let you come anyway.

The earth does not lie, but it does not choose. I choose.

A red crystal came into our earth not long ago, from a broken land far beyond this sky, a land of demons. It is new, and strange, and strong, and the earth does not yet know what it will do. You are like that crystal.

[after: 1374] Khan Jehn leaned on his strength as on a wall, and the earth is patient with walls. Your hands brought it down, for us.

When your people come to break the other clans, let them leave the Gelkis standing. That is all I ask of them. Of you, I ask nothing more. You are a friend. I say it knowing what I know.
