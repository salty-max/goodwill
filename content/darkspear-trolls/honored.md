Off the coast, past Sen'jin Village, lie the Echo Isles. That was meant to be our home in this land.

A witch doctor went bad out there. Zalazane. He took the isles for himself, and he took trolls with them, our own, hexed so deep they don't know their names. They walk his beaches with empty faces and do what he says.

Now and then one of them comes back to us. How, I couldn't tell you. My grandmother said the hex wears thin with distance, like a rope. They come to the village with nothing. Not even their own minds, at first. We wrap them up. We bring them to the fire. We say their names to them till they say them back.

A good deal of what comes to this desk goes to that. Blankets for trolls who didn't know they were cold. The first warm night, most of them sleep through to noon, and nobody in the village wakes them.
