The loa notice everything, ya know. Every gift. Every theft.

The way my grandmother taught it, you bargain with them. A little smoke, a little of what you love best, and the loa give back what they please. The Darkspear brought the old loa across the sea with us, and they came, and they're here, in the smoke of the village fires.

The gifts at this desk, they notice. Vehena knows, because the fires burn easier on the nights after cloth comes in. Maybe that's the loa. Maybe that's just more blankets, so more trolls sleep warm and nobody has to stay up feeding the fire. Both is true, I think. The loa like it when both is true.

I made a bargain myself, when I was young. A little of what I loved best, for a little luck. I gave them my singing voice. Never got it back. The luck, I'm still waiting on. Maybe it came wrapped in somebody's wool, and I didn't notice.
