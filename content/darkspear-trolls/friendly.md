Ah, now. Let me read you. No cloth in your hands? Then I'll read the hands. They been on a long road. There's dust on them from a hot place, and something colder under that, from before. You been fighting, too. The hands remember.

Vehena knows things.

A fisherman down at the village lost his sail in a squall last month. The first wool through my hands after went down the road to him, and his daughter stitched it on, and now there's a boat going out every morning under a stranger's gift. We Darkspear are sea folk now. A boat without a sail is a troll without a supper.

[after: 7833] That was your wool, as it happens. The boat goes out under a sail that smells faintly of you. The village thanks you. The loa, they're always grateful, but with the loa it always costs something.
