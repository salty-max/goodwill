Ah, now. Let me read you.

This wool, it's been on a long road. There's dust in it from a hot place, and something colder under that, from before. You carried it in your pack with your own clothes, so it smells of you. You been fighting, too. Not the cloth's fault, but it remembers.

Vehena knows things.

A fisherman down at the village lost his sail in a squall last month. Your wool was the first cloth through my hands after, so your wool went down the road to him, and his daughter stitched it on, and now there's a boat going out every morning under a sail that smells faintly of you. We Darkspear are sea folk now. A boat without a sail is a troll without a supper.

The village thanks you. The loa, they're always grateful, but with the loa it always costs something.
