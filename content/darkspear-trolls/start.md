Come, come. Bring that here, let Vehena have a feel of it.

Every piece of cloth got a story in it, ya know. Where the sheep grazed, what the spider ate, who carried it and how far. Most folk can't hear it. Vehena can. A gift from the loa, my grandmother said, or a curse, depending on the cloth.

The Darkspear need cloth. Bandages, wraps, sails, blankets for the little ones down at Sen'jin Village, on the coast. Bring me some, and I'll tell you where you been. Free of charge. The loa don't like it when I charge.
