I know your name.

Do not gloat. It is the first mortal name I have troubled to keep in an age, and I have filed it. For me that is as close as affection comes. I have filed it somewhere distinguished. You would not believe the company. Generals, poets, and one very tiresome queen.

The entry is short. Your name, and beneath it a note in my own hand. It began as a single word, "persistent." Later I added "useful," and then, after a long argument with myself, "welcome." I crossed that last word out once. It came back, and I have let it stay.

[after: 7508] You brought me back the dull old blade, and I told you what it would take to give it its edge again, dragon and all. I expected to see neither of you again. I was wrong, about you at least, and I am rarely wrong, and I have written that down too.

I have read nearly everything this house ever set down. Very little of it surprised me, and none of it lately. You did, a little, and more than once. Go and do something worth adding. I will be here with the index, being unbearable, and I have left room after your name.
