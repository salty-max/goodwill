The index is for something. Nobody has asked me what in a very long time.

Everything this house has known since before the world broke is set down somewhere in these halls, and I know where. Every war, every spell, every foolish prince, every name. I keep the index so that if the Shen'dralar are one day gone, and I suspect that day is nearer than the Prince believes, what we knew will not go with us. The Prince likes to say he keeps the house alive. I keep what it knew. Of the two, mine will last longer.

The Athenaeum is vast, and that is the least of it. It waits, for readers who never come. Bring me back one of our tomes and I may tear you out a page. Yes, I, who hold that nothing written is worthless. A page in a book nobody opens might as well be furniture. I would rather lose a page to a reader than a book to the dust. A mortal who carries one page out of here might one day come back for the rest.
