You insist on being underfoot, so you may as well know the house you are walking through.

Master Winthalus sobbed over a lost libram. Sobbed. Over a protective cantrip. I have shed tears over a lost book myself. Losing what was known is the only grief I respect. But one weeps for a history, not for a party trick.

The Prince had him flayed. I called it a favor at the time, and I have had a long while to wonder whether I meant it. That tells you something about this house, or about me, and I would rather not look into it.

Then there is Azj'Tordin, who ran from the Prince, and an imp relieved him of his book of spells on the way out. He will die now, like any of you. And for what? Some fresh air and a great many trees.

You are still here. How odd. Most visitors have the sense to leave by now.
