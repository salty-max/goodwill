The librams, then. Those are what I want from you.

Some years ago an elf of Quel'Thalas came to us, Master Winthalus, fleeing the ruin of his homeland with a few old treasures. He then mislaid them about these halls with a carelessness I can only call heroic. Each libram holds a little spell for your gear. Party tricks. Still, bring me one with the reagents it asks, and I will make you its arcanum, and you may wear it and feel very slightly less mortal for an afternoon.

Do not mistake my tone. The librams bore me, and I want every one of them back. Nothing written is worthless. It is the one belief I have kept whole in all my years, and I would thank you not to test it.

Who are you again? It is coming to me.
