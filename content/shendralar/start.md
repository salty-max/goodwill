Ah. A visitor. How novel. No, do not tell me your name. I would only have to forget it, and that is such an effort, and the catalogue will not keep itself.

When one has kept the index of a house for ten thousand years, a mortal's hour grows very small, and your troubles, whatever they are, shrink with it. I say that without malice. I say most things without malice. It saves time.

Every tome in these halls bears the house's seal, and far too many of them are not where they belong. Bring one back, and I may begin to remember you.
