I saw you coming up the road. You want the tunnel. Strangers always want the tunnel, because it is shorter than the mountains. It is not yours.

We are the Timbermaw, the last tribe the corruption has not taken. You have met the others. Everyone has, and everyone shoots first now, without stopping to look. So do we.

Grazle will tell you how to change our minds. Grazle tells everyone everything. Do what he asks, and then do it again.

I will be here, at the door. I have one eye. For you, it is enough.
