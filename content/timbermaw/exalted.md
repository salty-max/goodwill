Walk slower through the hold from now on. You will, once you know why.

You think this place is a road. A good road, out of the snow, with furbolgs in it who have learned not to shoot you. It was never a road. The hold was made for Ursoc and Ursol, the twin bears we honour, and every tunnel you have hurried through to save yourself a mountain is part of it. We let those we trust walk through a holy place. Most of them never knew what it was. Now you do.

You will ask about the eye. Everyone who gets this far asks about the eye. No.

I was the last in this hold to trust you, and I would like that remembered, because I was right to be slow. Trust that comes easily leaves the same way. Mine came the slow way, and it is not going anywhere.

[after: 8481] There is one more thing, and we do not give it to many. In a bad hour, far from here, one of our ancestors will come and stand with you, and mend you while it fights. Not for small hours. They are old, and they are ours, and they come as a favour to me.
