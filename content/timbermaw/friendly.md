From here on, it is counting.

Feathers off the headdresses of the Deadwood, to Nafien at the gate or to Grazle, for those who bring them. They tell me the count whether I ask or not. I do not enjoy it. The Deadwood were our kin before the fel got into Felwood and into them, and a feather does not say which of them it was.

The beads are worse. The Winterfall carried them against foul spirits. You can see how well that went. They wore their wards, the foulness walked in anyway, and now their beads go to Salfa in strangers' bags.

Salfa thinks you are useful, and Salfa is seldom wrong. I still watch you.
