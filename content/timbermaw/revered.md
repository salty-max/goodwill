I will say this once, and you will not repeat it to Grazle.

We cannot hold this place alone. Salfa tells strangers so, because Salfa is honest to a fault. I say it to you because you are not a stranger any more, which I did not expect to be saying this year. If the Winterfall and the Deadwood came at us from two sides at once, we would need every friend we have, and there are not many.

Your kings have sent word to this hold before. The dwarf king under his mountain. The warchief of the orcs, in Durotar. They wanted to talk. There was no peace. I could give you the reasons, but you would not like them, and most of them would be about your people.

You are Revered among us. Here the word means something plainer than it does among you. It means that if you fell at the door, someone would come out for you. It would be me. I would complain the whole way.
