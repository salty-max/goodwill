You have walked the hold so often now that the guards no longer reach for anything when you pass. So take this, and do with it whatever your kind does with things it is told.

The night elves took our help once, against the demons, and many of them took it holding their noses. Some of them have not stopped. Ask the Sentinels, on a day they feel honest.

The Winterfall were our friends, and not long ago. Then their high chief began to want things nobody understood, and his tribe turned on everyone near it, us with the rest. Since then we keep to ourselves. We learned that much.

Your people write their history down. We do not. And what we hold dearest, you have walked through a hundred times without seeing. Not yet.
