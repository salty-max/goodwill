An insect. At my table. Speaking to me.

We have no business with insects, and I have no patience for them. You have nothing I want, you know nothing I need, and you are blocking my view of the door. The Brotherhood walked out on the emperor long ago. I still drink in his city. Neither is your business.

The Brotherhood keeps a house at Thorium Point for strays, where they are kinder. Be a stray there first. If they ever learn your name, come back, and I will consider learning it too.

Until then, away. Away.
