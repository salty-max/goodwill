The Brotherhood receives you, and acknowledges your... oh, sit, if you like. Nobody sits with me. That is how you know it is an honor.

You think we are Dark Irons who grew a conscience. Half of that is right. We were a guild before we were anything else, a craft guild in Ironforge, when there was one Ironforge and the clans had not yet gone to war over it. A frightened guild. We were sure our rivals knew secrets we did not. So we got hold of their best work and took it apart to see. That is how we found enchantment. Not by study. By theft, and patience, and a very small knife.

When the war came and the clans chose sides, we chose. You know which. I will not apologize for it to a stranger, and I am still deciding what you are, so I will not apologize to you either.

Our best secret, we stole first.
