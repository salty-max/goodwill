The Brotherhood will hear your question. No. I will.

You want to know why we left. Not the short version.

When the Fire Lord woke, the mountain was his, and so were we. The emperor's people became his servants, and the Brotherhood became his smiths. We worked his forges. We made his weapons, the same blade over and over, for an army that did not care how well a thing was made. Understand me. It was not the cruelty. Dwarves can bear cruelty. It was the waste. The finest hands under the mountain, hammering out the same sword for creatures who could not tell it from a worse one.

It was my apprentice who found the way out. Oilfist. Overseer Oilfist now, at Thorium Point, running the place like a garrison. He planned it under the overseers' noses, and we made what we needed to walk out of the Depths. Most who got free ran as far as their legs would carry them. We stopped across the Gorge. The materials are here. Craft does not run.

And why am I here, at a table in the emperor's own city? The ale. Next question.
