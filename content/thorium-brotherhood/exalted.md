No more ceremony. You and I are past weighing offerings. Listen, plainly, because this is the only way I talk to you now.

The Brotherhood holds the secret of the Fire Lord's hammer. Sulfuras. The great maul he carries down there in the Core. We can teach a smith to forge the Sulfuron hammer, and with the Eye of Sulfuras set into it, a hero could carry the Hand of Ragnaros out into the world. Think about what that means. We spent an age making weapons for him. Now we sell the making of his own to anyone strong enough to kill him, at a fair price. I have never been so pleased with a piece of business in my life.

I lose my head about it. Put a Sulfuron ingot in front of me and I will shout. I know. I do not care.

Here is the other thing, since you are past the door. A guild like ours does not live on craft alone. When a rival grows too good, now and then, his forge has an accident. We are never anywhere near it. You will remember that we are never anywhere near it.

You came to my table an insect. You leave it a partner. Read your contracts, all of them. Mine especially.
