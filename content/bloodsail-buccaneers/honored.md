Honored, is it. Precious few on this ship ever got so far with us, and most of them were born to the trade.

I took ye on as a knife. A knife's a useful thing, and ye're careful with it, and ye come back. These days I'd trust ye with the wheel, and I don't let my own mother near the wheel.

[after: 4621] Ye did what I asked, both of them, and walked out of the bay to tell it. The hat suits ye, Admiral. There's not another like it on the sea. Every time ye cross the deck in it, the crew stands up straighter, and they've no idea they're doing it.

Some go back, ye know. Every few seasons one of mine gets homesick for clean sheets and goblin ale, and the bay takes them in again, for a price, and we call them traitors after. I don't blame them much. The sea's hard on folk.

Before they go, I give them the same advice. A goblin's forgiveness costs coin. The sea's costs nothing, and it never once asked me to wipe my boots.
