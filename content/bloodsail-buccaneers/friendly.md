title: The Two Throats
beside: [bloodsail-buccaneers, booty-bay]

Well now. The bay's bruisers reach for their clubs when ye walk in. Good. That's the first honest welcome a goblin ever gave anybody.

Ye bled the bay a long while to get here, and ye know what it cost. Every goblin town has yer name on a list by now, and it's not the list ye'd want.

[after: 9272] Ye wear the colors well. The man who had them before never filled them out, and the storm has him now.

The bay has two throats that matter. Fleet Master Seahorn, who keeps their ships on the water, and the Baron, who keeps their law on the land. Cut both, and the goblins learn there's a flag on this sea they can't buy.
