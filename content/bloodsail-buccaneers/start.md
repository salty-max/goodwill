Ye smell of the bay. Steamwheedle coin and goblin soap.

Every soul who runs errands for Revilgaz is an enemy of the Bloodsail, and we've a long memory for enemies and no patience for them. I've known plenty start where ye stand, fetching for the goblins, till the day they set what the bay pays beside what the sea pays and did the sum.

The sea's a wide place. The bay's the only thing on it that tells a Bloodsail where he may not sail. Some day ye may tire of the goblins. There's a red ship that takes on hands, and nobody aboard her asks where ye've been.
