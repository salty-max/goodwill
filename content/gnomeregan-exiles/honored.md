I keep a list. Don't laugh. Well, laugh a little.

It's every address in Gnomeregan. Every street, every level, every workshop and flat and storeroom, and whose they were. I wrote it down from memory the first winter, and then I went round the exiles one by one and asked them where they'd lived, and wrote that down too. Some of them cried. Some of them corrected me. One of them corrected me and cried.

Whoever walks those streets again, whenever that is, won't have to stand wondering which door was theirs. I'll have it written down. They'll go in the right door and find whatever's left, and it'll be theirs.

That's what all this is for, really. The cloth keeps us alive till then. The list keeps us us. I keep it in a box lined with the first bolt anyone ever gave me here.
