Come here. Mind the needle.

This is a banner. Or it will be, when it's done. It's for whatever day we next have something worth celebrating, because on a day like that somebody ought to be carrying something soft at the front, and I've decided it's going to be me.

It's made of scraps. One from everything that's been donated at this table, since the first winter. Wool and silk and mageweave and runecloth, all stitched together. That patch there came from a gnome who walked out of the city with nothing else. That one's a sleeve off somebody's best coat. Most of the border came from folk who've never set foot in Gnomeregan. I didn't plan it that way. It just kept happening. Every stitch I've done at night, after the desk closes, with the lamp turned low. It's the best thing I've made since the workshop, and this one I'm not going to forget.

I haven't got a joke for this. I've tried. I keep starting one and then stopping, because it's only true. The cloth at this table helped us stay alive long enough to have something to celebrate. When that day comes, you'll be out in the wind with the rest of us.

There. That's the banner. Now help me thread this, my hands aren't what they were.
