Ah! A customer! Well, a donor. Well, a potential donor, which is the very best kind, because potential is a thing gnomes understand!

We exiles are in a spot, and there's no dressing it up. Short on cloth, short on room, short on everything except ideas, and you can't wrap a bandage round an idea. Believe me, I've tried. It came out as a schematic.

Bring me cloth, any cloth, and the gnomes will think well of you. All of us, everywhere. That's not a small thing. Gnomes are everywhere, and we have long memories, and we write everything down.
