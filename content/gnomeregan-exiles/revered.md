Mine was on a lower level, past the big gears. Not grand. A bench, a lamp, a shelf of parts I was sure I'd need one day and never did. They're probably still there, being unnecessary in the dark.

There was something on the bench when we left. Half done. I'd been working on it for a year, and it was going to be the best thing I ever made, and I can't tell you what it was. Not because it's a secret. Because I've forgotten. I've tried and tried, and I remember the bench and the lamp, and the thing on it is just a shape.

That's the part nobody warns you about. You think you'll lose the city all at once, the day you leave. You don't. You lose it a little at a time, after, in your head. That's why the list matters so much to me, and why I'll keep a gnome's curtain on it as carefully as a gnome's door.
