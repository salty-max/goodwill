title: Why the Dead Need Binding
beside: [undercity, forsaken, tirisfal]

Cloth keeps coming to this desk. Very well. You ought to know what it is for.

The living heal. They cut themselves, and in a week there is a scar, and they forget. We heal slowly and badly, and never all the way. The apothecaries can stitch and salve and slow the rot, but what the body loses, it is slow to give back. So we bind it. We wrap what is loose, and hold what is open, and keep ourselves together with cloth, the way you would keep a cracked jar together with cord.

A Forsaken soldier goes through bandages faster than any of the living, and he uses them to hold himself together.

The clerks find the question of why the dead need so much linen tiresome. So do I. Ask it again, and I will have you entered in the ledger under nuisances.
