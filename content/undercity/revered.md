Most of what comes across this desk comes from the dead. We look after our own. But some of it comes from the living. Orcs, trolls, tauren.

They carry something in with them that they do not notice. Warmth, truly, off their hands and their breath, and on the cloth for a little while after they set it down. I can feel it, if I am quick. It fades in a minute or two. Forsaken hands carry nothing of the kind. I have checked my own. Trolls run cool, orcs run hot, and the tauren warmest of all.

I used to be warm. I remember it the way you might remember a language you spoke as a child. I could not hold a conversation in it now. But I know it when I hear it.

Your cloth is the only cloth I check twice, whatever it carries. I do not tell the other clerks that I wait, after you leave, before I put it away.
