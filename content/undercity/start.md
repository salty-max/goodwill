You there. Listen closely. I dislike saying things twice.

The Forsaken need cloth. Wool, silk, whatever you happen to be carrying. Leave it with the clerks. They will weigh it, and enter it in a ledger that nobody reads but me, and I read it every night, line by line.

Nothing left at this desk goes unrecorded. Nothing given here goes unnoticed. Most people find that reassuring. Some find it a threat. Both are correct, and I leave you to decide which you are.
