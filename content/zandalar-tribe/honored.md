You have never eaten an essence mango. Nobody who has tasted one asks what it is.

They grow wherever the South Seas have a beach to grow on. One of them and you are rested, body and thought together, as if you had slept a night on the sand at home. I keep a supply here on the isle for those the tribe has come to respect. I could sell them here for a small fortune. I do not. Some fruit is only for friends.

I do not miss Zuldazar for its king or its priests. I miss it for the fruit, and the sea, and my own language spoken by people who are not afraid. Here everyone is afraid. You can hear it in how quietly the isle talks once the sun is down. Here even the fruit tastes a long way from home.
