Welcome. They call me Rin'wosho, and I trade out of Zuldazar, a long way from home and further still from my best customers.

You will want to see what I carry. Everyone does. Goods of the South Seas, patterns for your tailors and your leatherworkers, fruit you have never tasted. And I would love to sell them to you. But I barter only with friends of my tribe, and you are not one yet.

The Zandalar are at war with a god who should have stayed gone. Help them in that, then come back, and we will talk prices.
