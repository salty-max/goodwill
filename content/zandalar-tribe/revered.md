With your standing come my most potent brews, the ones Zanza has blessed. Take one at a time, my friend. A blessing is not a thing you stack.

Why does the tribe hand its blessings to outsiders at all? In Zul'Gurub the high priests are the Soulflayer's thralls, and through them he drinks our gods, their strength rather than their blood. Every day he holds that city, the oldest powers my people know grow a little weaker, and he a little stronger, and no one can tell me where the bottom of that lies.

I sleep badly on this isle. A merchant knows what leaves his shelves, and I can no longer reckon what leaves the gods. Some nights I walk the beach and talk to the sea in my own language, so that something on this isle still hears it.

So when I hand you a blessed brew, call it stock moved before the warehouse burns.
