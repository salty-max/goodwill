Now we can do business, my friend.

Among the Zandalar it goes like this. What you carry out of Zul'Gurub for the tribe, the tribe repays in honor, in tokens to keep and to spend with me. I like a people whose gratitude fits in a purse. Spend them or keep them, as you please. I have known people keep one for years, for no reason they could name.

The coins the trolls of that city carry are another matter. Each of those peoples has its own, every one of them thick with mojo, and Vinchaxa takes them for the tribe's use. I would not haggle over those. Some things are worth more than they buy.
