Now we can do business, my friend.

Among the Zandalar it goes like this. What you carry out of Zul'Gurub for the tribe, the tribe repays in honour, in tokens to keep and to spend with me. I like a people whose gratitude fits in a purse. Spend them or keep them, as you please. I have known people keep one for years, for no reason they could name.

The trolls of Zul'Gurub carry coins of their own, one kind for each of its tribes, every one thick with mojo, and Vinchaxa takes them for the tribe's use. I would not haggle over those. Some things are worth more than they buy.
