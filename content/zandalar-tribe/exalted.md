These I sell to no one else. Signets, carried across the water from the South Seas, for the shoulders of those the tribe counts as its own. Might, mojo or serenity: I have fitted a great many shoulders in my life, and few of them carried anything worth the weight.

Under every trade we have made lies one plain thing. The coins go into the tribe's defense against the god in that city, and the bijous are broken on Zanza's altar, every scrap of mojo turned against him. For a long time I told myself I was only selling. I stopped believing it some while ago. I think it was about when I began keeping the best mangoes back for you.

I will go home one day, when the Soulflayer is ash and our gods are whole again. The sea will still be there. It is the one thing I have never had to bargain for. The first evening, I will eat a mango on the sand and think of this ugly little island, and I suspect I will miss it. When I go, I will tell them about you, and about your bargains.
