<Laggrond salutes you.>

You came back from the field, and you came back a soldier. I know it by how you stand.

[after: 7163] Take this insignia, and wear it always. It says friend of the clan.

That heat in your hands out there, the first time the horns blew and your feet moved before you told them to, that is your spirit answering. Some call that fear. It is the oldest part of you, remembering what it is for.

The clan has a word for a soldier who comes back like that: friend. It does not give that word lightly. The clan is small, and old in these mountains, and it remembers every hand that was raised for it.
