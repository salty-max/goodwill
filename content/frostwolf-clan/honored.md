My people never drank the demon's blood. I am proud of that every day of my life.

And still, when the horns sound in this valley, something in me answers, and I do not like the answer. The battle rage. It climbs slowly in an old orc, but it climbs. My pulse heavy in my ears, the cold gone from my hands. For a while there is nothing but the next blow, and the next, and it is the best I have felt in years.

That is why I keep it on a short rope. A clan that refused the demon must not come to love the fury for its own sake. I tell this to every soldier who reaches this rank, and most of them forget it by the next battle.

[after: 7164] The next insignia is yours. Keep the rope short, and remember who is holding it.
