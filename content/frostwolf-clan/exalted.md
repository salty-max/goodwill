Drek'Thar speaks of you. Out loud, to the elders, where everyone can hear. One of the elders asked me afterward whether you were really as cold in battle as the soldiers say. I told him colder.

The Eye of Command is yours. With it, the soldiers of the valley will follow your word. They will go where you send them, and they will trust that you meant to send them there.

When the horns blow, the red comes up in most of us. In you it goes quiet, and you weigh the towers and the keep and the ground between, and choose. That is rarer than courage, and far rarer than rage. I have known a great deal of both, and the rage I have known from the inside.

One thing more, with the Eye. You know what a battle costs the ones who carry the banner, and you go anyway. Go, then, and bring home as many as you can. The young ones will follow you into anything now, and when they get there, they should find it was worth the going. An orc who has followed a good leader once will follow the next one better.

<Laggrond salutes.>

A cold head and a warm heart. The Frostwolf ask for no more than that.
