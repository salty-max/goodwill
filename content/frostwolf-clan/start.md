You have come to the valley. Good. Out of the wind, stranger.

For a long while there was no war here. The Frostwolves hunted, and raised their young, and buried their old, and the mountains kept us. Now the dwarves have come over the mountains with their banners, and the horns sound again in the valley, and every orc of this clan feels it in the chest.

Nothing on this field is handed out. What honour you have, you carry off it yourself. Fight for the Frostwolf, and come back to me, and I will learn your name.
