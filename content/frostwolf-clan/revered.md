<Laggrond roars.>

They are telling tales of you, soldier. In the keep, around the fires, on the towers. Even across the snow, they say, the dwarves go quiet at your name.

Tales are a good thing, for a clan. They make the young ones brave. Nobody told me what they do to the one they are told about. They make you want more of them. The next fight bigger, the next tale louder. A warrior who fights for the tale is one charge away from a grave nobody remembers.

The clan still tells one about a young orc who went into the snow alone after a great bear and came home with its hide. It does not tell that the bear's mate came down the pass while he knelt over the hide, admiring it, and that his brother, who had followed him out, stood in her way. The brother lived. He never walked the same again. I was the young orc. When the clan tells it at the fire, I tell the second half, and my brother nods.

Keep the tale small.
