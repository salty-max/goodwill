Hail, $gbrother:sister;. Walk with me a moment, if the wind allows it.

My people have walked a long way to stand on these bluffs, and the walking was not easy. Now that we have stopped, we find we need things we never needed on the road. Cloth is one of them. We are short of it, badly, and I am the one who must ask.

I do not ask easily. You will forgive me if I am slow about it. If you have cloth to spare, the tauren would take it gratefully. A kindness to one of us is owed by all of us, for as long as the bluffs stand.
