title: The Earth Mother's Gifts

We believe everything comes from the Earth Mother. The grass the herds eat, the herds themselves, the hides we wore, the flax we are learning to grow. A gift, always. Given freely, and repaid in care.

So when I take cloth from you, I take it as a gift that passed through your hands from hers. The sheep that grew the wool, the spider that spun the silk, the hands that wove it, and yours, that carried it here. That is a great many hands. I try to honor each of them.

I have come to think that this desk is a small prayer. A small one. The shamans make the grand ones, and say them better. They usually do. This is only a tauren standing in one place, taking what is given and passing it on to those who need it, the way the land does. When the shamans pray, the sky listens. When I pray, a child gets a blanket. I would not trade.

You have been a large part of that prayer, whether you meant to pray or not.
