beside: [stormwind-city, humans]

Every piece handed across this desk goes somewhere, friend, and I make it my business to know where. The orphanage, for one, by the Cathedral. The children of soldiers live there. Last winter a good deal of donated silk went to their blankets, and I went along with the cart, to check the paperwork. The matron counted every blanket against my list. Then the children took them, and wrapped themselves up, and went quiet in a way I have never managed to make a council go quiet.

I have been asked, more than once, whether a city with soldiers to feed ought to spend its cloth on blankets for orphans. I always say that a city with soldiers has more orphans than anyone, and that a city that stops spending on them has forgotten what the soldiers are for.

[after: 7791] Some of that silk was yours. The ledger never lies.
