title: Where It Goes
beside: [stormwind-city, humans]

Every piece you hand across this desk goes somewhere, friend, and I make it my business to know where. The orphanage, for one, by the Cathedral. The children of soldiers live there, and the city has a great many soldiers. Last winter your silk went to their blankets, and I went along with the cart, purely to check the paperwork, you understand. The matron counted every blanket against my list. Then the children took them, and wrapped themselves up, and went quiet in a way I have never managed to make a council go quiet.

I have been asked, more than once, whether a city with soldiers to feed ought to spend its cloth on blankets for orphans. I always say that a city with soldiers has more orphans than anyone, and that a city that stops spending on them has forgotten what the soldiers are for.
