Friend! Yes, you. Stormwind needs you, or rather it needs your cloth, which is very nearly the same thing!

Our stores of textiles have fallen to a state that frankly alarms me. Bandages, tunics, blankets, every one of them begins as cloth, and the city goes through it faster than anyone will admit at a council meeting. So we have an effort. A great, many-sided, thoroughly organized effort, and I have the honour of running it.

Wool, silk, whatever you take off the bandits on the road. Leave it on the desk and I will see to the rest, friend! The citizens notice who gives. So do I. I keep very good records.
