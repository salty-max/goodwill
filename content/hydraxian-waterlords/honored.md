They spoke of you in our chambers, and at length. That is not our way. You were the subject, not the judge, and it is right that you hear how it went.

Some among us thought you a poor thing to spend on our war, a creature we would pour strength into only for it to crack. They were not wrong, and they are not wrong now. You are small. You do break. You came back anyway, against our enemies, again and again, and each return made their view a little harder to hold.

None of us thought it possible. The Waterlords respect you now. It troubles the order of things in a way I have no word for, because a small thing should not weigh this much, and you do. The doubters have gone quiet, the way a current goes still before it turns.
