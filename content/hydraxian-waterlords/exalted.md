Gratitude is a mortal habit. Among us, nothing is owed for work well done. The current does its work and no one praises it for that. The sea does not thank the river for arriving.

You have fought for us with a steadiness I did not think a creature of your size could keep, and I want to give you something. I tried to think what one of you would want, and could not. So I give you what we would want, if we wanted anything. It is as much thanks as the deep can give, and I am told that among your kind it is customary to say so aloud, so I have. Take it, then: a source of our quintessence that will not run dry. I do not set a term.

[after: 6824] You brought me the hands of the Firelord's captains. Word of it ran down through our deep halls like a current. I did not think one so small could become a legend here. You are one already.

You are small, and you will not last. I still think both of those things. The deep forgets almost everything. It has decided to keep you.
