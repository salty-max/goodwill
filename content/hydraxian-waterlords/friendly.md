You have been useful. That is rarer than you would think, among your kind.

Understand what we fight for. You are beside us now. What makes us strong is that we are pure. Water that takes in whatever it touches is no longer water, only a sickness that flows. When the plague of Lordaeron got into the elementals we sent there, what offended me was what they had become.

Of one thing I know nothing. Our enemies of air and earth guard objects of power in the south, away from mortals, and I cannot tell you why. I know only that whatever they keep, I would rather they did not.
