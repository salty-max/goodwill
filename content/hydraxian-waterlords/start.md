You were not born in water, and what we fight over will always be closed to you. Nothing here will be made clearer by pretending.

A bargain needs no understanding. The Waterlords fight the lords of air, of earth and of fire, and it is fire we cannot forgive. You are small and short-lived, and you walk on land that burns. You have reasons of your own to want the fire put out. Your reasons and ours may run the same way for a while. I mean to use that.

Prove useful, and the deep will remember you. It forgets almost everything.
