The water has no mercy and no malice. I will speak the way it does.

It costs us to lose a hand that has proved itself, and you have. To send one of you against the Firelord's own captains is the hardest thing we ask, and we ask it only when the gain is judged worth the loss. That judgment is the highest compliment the Waterlords know how to pay. I would not blame you for not hearing it as one. We have spent agents before, and the water closed over them. It would not close so quickly over you.

Beneath the mountain the floor is cut with runes of fire. Each one is a door to its realm, and each one feeds it. When a rune goes out, one door shuts, the fire draws a little less from home, and it is a little more alone in the world. That is all we want of it: that it be alone, and then that it be gone.
