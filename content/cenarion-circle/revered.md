What keeps me up at night is a short list. It keeps me up, so it can keep you up too.

The cult isn't only praying out there. They're calling things up out of the summoning stones: templars, then dukes, then lords, each one bigger than the last and each one a bigger headache for me. And each one hands down orders. We know they can be smashed. What we don't know is who they answer to. Maybe the cult worships them. Maybe they're only messengers, and whatever sends the messages hasn't shown itself yet.

So, the list. The bugs in their hives and behind the wall. The cult in the desert. Whatever the cult calls up. Whatever has Huum so quiet in the evenings. That's a lot of enemies for one stretch of sand.

Everyone tells me to wait and watch. I've been waiting and watching a long time. It hasn't grown on me.
