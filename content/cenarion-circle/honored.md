Here. You've earned a story, and I've got one, and it's mostly about my brother.

My people, I always say, do things slowly, listening first. Huum is one of my people all the way through. Huum listens first. He says the spirits here are calling him, and the sand is grieving over something it needs someone to hear.

I don't hear it. I've tried, hard, the way I try everything. I stand where he stands and all I get is sand in my ears. But I believe him. Because he's my brother. Because I've never once managed to prove him wrong. And because when he goes quiet in the evening, he stays quiet too long.

So I fight, and he listens, and between the two of us the Hold has what it needs.
