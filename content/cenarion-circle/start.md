Welcome to the Hold! Everybody here watches the bugs. They're big, there are a great many of them, and they sit in their hives and behind that wall as if they're waiting for something, so I don't blame anyone for watching.

But turn around. The cultists out in the desert aren't waiting. They're building, and calling, and passing each other notes in scribble. My big brother Huum and I stay on them, because somebody has to and nobody else will.

Bugs, we've got plenty. A real fight, I've got better.
