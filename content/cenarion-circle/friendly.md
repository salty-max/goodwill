The cult's papers are what I want most. Little scraps covered in scribble. That's how their camps talk to each other!

I can't read them, and I don't need to. Every stack taken off a dead cultist is a message that never arrives, an order nobody gets, a camp waiting for word and hearing nothing. That's a fight too. A quiet one, but a fight.

Huum would want to read them. Huum listens to things, the land, the spirits, papers too, I expect. Me, I stash them. Someday someone clever will want them, and until then every scrap in my stash is one the cult never gets to read.
