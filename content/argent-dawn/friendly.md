You have been about the Plaguelands long enough now that we may talk as colleagues.

Do you know what they carry, the dead? Insignia. The Scourge is an army, and an army has ranks, even when its soldiers are dead. The common stones mark its rank and file. The rarer mark its elite. The rarest belong to its masters, the creatures that give the orders, and they are worth a great deal more than the rest. I keep the count. Some nights it is the only proof I have that we are winning.

Every set brought to me earns a token. Somewhere in the Plaguelands, a thing that once took orders has stopped taking them. That gives me a great deal of satisfaction. I suspect that says something about me, and I have no intention of finding out what.
