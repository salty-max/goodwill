Come in, come in! No, stand there, in the light, where I can see you properly.

You have the look of someone who can swing a weapon and has not yet been told what for. That is where I come in. The Dawn has one enemy, the dead, and no interest at all in which of the living you came from. Take our commission. What the Scourge carries on it is of great interest to me, and then we shall know what you are.

One more thing. Everything in this world that is pushed too far becomes its own enemy. Courage. Faith. The Light itself. Remember that. I shall ask you about it later.
