You will have noticed that I am always here when you return. I send. That is what I do. I send people like you into Stratholme and the dead fields, and I wait at Light's Hope and count what comes back, the stones and the people. The stones always come back.

So I keep two counts. The stones go in the ledger, where the quartermasters can see them. The other count I keep nowhere at all, and I could recite every line of it to you.

You will also have noticed that I never call the Scourge's rank and file weak. I learned why from the ones who did not come back.

Naxxramas is up there, Kel'Thuzad's citadel, and the Dawn will send people into it. Some of them will be people I sent somewhere else first.

[after: 5262] And you brought me a demon's head out of Stratholme, and I have not yet decided what it means. Those people in red believed they served the Light. They were serving a dreadlord. I called them blasphemers. I should like to know what to call them now.
