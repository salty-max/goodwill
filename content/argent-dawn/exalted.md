Champion.

Kneel. No, do not kneel. I am not a king, and you would only get up again with that look on your face.

I have not called anyone that in years. I should like you to understand that I do not use the word loosely, because I do not use anything loosely, as you may have noticed.

I told you once that everything pushed too far becomes its own enemy. I have thought about it a great deal since, which is your fault. I am a man who would burn a library for the Light and call the smoke a sermon. If anyone in this chapel is in danger of becoming what he hates, my friend, it is not you.

That is why I need you. A champion is not someone who agrees with me. A champion is someone I trust to tell me when I have gone too far, and whom I will believe. I have not had one for a long time.

The war is not over. One day the Dawn will go against the master of all this, wherever he sits, and on that day I should like you near me. Not in front. Near.
