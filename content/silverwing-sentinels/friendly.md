You have been in the gulch.

[after: 8289] Each talisman you bring me is a fight you lived through. I hold it a moment before it goes with the others. My sisters call it a ritual. I like to know what a thing weighs before I trust it, and a talisman weighs exactly what it cost.

The orcs are loud, and they like it that way. I have never needed to match them.

My sisters say I talk to the trees. I do not. I listen to them, which is different, and they have more to say than most of the people who pass me on this road.
