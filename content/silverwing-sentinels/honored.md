I walked this valley when it was forest.

There was an oak at the far end, where the orcs' mill is now, older than anything I knew. A family of owls lived in it. I lived a whole season in its branches once, doing nothing at all. Nobody was in a hurry then.

The axes came at night, the first time. By morning there was a track through the ferns, and by the end of that year there was a mill. The owls left the spring after. I do not know where they went. I have listened for them at every old oak between here and Astranaar, and I have not heard them yet.

Much of the valley is stumps now. The orcs say they needed the wood. Perhaps they did. I have not forgiven it, and I do not mean to. It is quiet in me, and it will outlast the war.
