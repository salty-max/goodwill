You are of the Alliance. That will do for the gulch.

I am Farsong. I keep the road to it, with my sisters of the Silverwing. The orcs keep the road on the other side. Between us is a valley that used to be forest, and a great deal of fighting.

I do not say much. You will get used to it. Fight beside us, and bring me what you earn in there, and we will find out whether you stay. Trust is slow, in Ashenvale. The trees taught us that.
