You find us searching. An order of scholars that has lost most of what it knew does little else.

We are alone now. The winds that carried my people into Skywall are gone, and I say so because most of the island still cannot. We were Highborne scholars once, and somewhere in the long years since, we forgot how to do anything the winds did not do for us. The High Order intends to remember.

There is work here for anyone willing to look.
