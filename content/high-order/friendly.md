Some come to the High Order born to it, some by choice, and I have stopped asking which. What matters is what you will do with it.

Here is what we do. We look for what our ancestors knew before the winds made knowing unnecessary: the arcane of Eldre'Thalas, a strength that does not depend on anyone's kindness. An archmage of the Kirin Tor in Dalaran answers my letters, which is more than the winds have done in a long time. Azeroth went on learning while we drifted. It will teach us, if we are humble enough to ask.

The Windshapers think us reckless. You will hear them say so. Hear them out, then come back here and do the work anyway.

[after: 92834] I sent Belathaan to the shrine to talk. I was certain talk was possible. I have been certain of very little since, and I find I work better that way.
