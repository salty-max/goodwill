I ran, once.

The night Stromgarde's walls were lost, I was on them. I had a post and a captain and a sword I knew how to use, and when the gate went, I ran. Down the hill, into the dark, and on until the noise stopped behind me. I've said since that the wall was already lost, and it was. One more sword would have been one more body. I ran because I was young and frightened, and the dark at the bottom of the hill felt like living.

A field marshal's record says where he stood. Mine says Stromgarde, and leaves the hill out.

The reports come up to me every evening from the basin, who held and who broke. And the recruits who held a point beside you hold the next one too. That spreads, in an army. Fear spreads, and so does staying put.

If the League ever holds the basin for good, I mean to go up the hill to the keep, and be first onto the wall. Somebody of Stromgarde ought to stand on it to the end this time, and I'd like it to be the one who ran.
