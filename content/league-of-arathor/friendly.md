You've been in the basin, then, and had a taste of what it's for.

We fight over what the land can carry. Every point the League holds is a road open to the front, with something going up it to an army that needs it. Every point the enemy holds is a road shut, and the same things going the other way.

The carts are moving our way these days. The quartermasters noticed before I did. They're the first to know when a cart is late, so they're the first to know when it isn't.

Keep at it. The League needs fighters who understand that holding a mill can matter as much as winning a duel. Most learn it the hard way. A few never do, and I write to their families.
