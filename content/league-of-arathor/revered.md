Stormwind pays for us. That's no secret. The coin, the arms, the horses, the bread: our benefactors' purse.

What the benefactors expect for it, I've my suspicions. I've seen how deeds get signed, by men who never stood in Arathi in the rain, and I don't think the people who farmed here before the war will get their fields back the way they had them. Some of those families are here at Refuge Pointe, mending my soldiers' boots, and they don't ask me about it.

I've made my peace with that, mostly. A farm feeds people whoever holds the deed. The people of Stromgarde will eat from it either way, and that's what I swore.

So fight for the people who'll eat from those farms, and leave the deeds to the men who sign them. A clerk would tell you a prettier story. I keep to this one myself, on the days I manage it.
