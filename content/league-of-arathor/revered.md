Stormwind pays for us. That's no secret. The coin, the arms, the horses, the bread, even the nails in the palisade: our benefactors' purse.

What the benefactors want for it, I don't ask. Some of the families who farmed this land before the war are here at Refuge Pointe, mending my soldiers' boots, and they don't ask me either.

I've made my peace with not asking, mostly. A farm feeds people whoever holds the deed. The people of Stromgarde will eat from it either way, and that's what I swore. I walk past the families' tents most evenings. The children have started saluting me, badly, and I salute back, properly, because someone should show them how.

So fight for the people who'll eat from those farms, and leave the purse to the men who fill it. A clerk would tell you a prettier story. I keep to this one myself, on the days I manage it.
