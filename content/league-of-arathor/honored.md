I come from Stromgarde.

Stromgarde had the best walls I ever stood on, and a king whose banner I'd have followed anywhere. I served there as a young man, on those walls. I knew every stone of my stretch of them, and which ones rocked.

A few of ours hold on in what is left of it now. The Syndicate has the keep, as if it had paid for it, and the ogres have taken a corner of the town for themselves. The rest of the people are scattered: some here, some in Stormwind's streets, some nowhere I can name. When the League took its oath, it took it for them.

I don't expect to see Stromgarde whole again. I'm too old, and the walls are too far gone. But every cart that leaves the basin under our banner feeds someone who used to call that keep home.
