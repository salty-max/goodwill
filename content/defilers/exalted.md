I said spite, when you were new to us. It's true, and it's the answer I like best to give, because it frightens the living and costs me nothing.

There's another, underneath.

When my people went looking for a place in the world, every door in it was shut against us but one, and it was the Horde's. The Horde took its time with us. Some of the orcs wanted nothing to do with the dead. Some of the tauren spoke for us anyway, and the Warchief heard them. Nobody else did, and I haven't forgotten who did.

Everything the Defilers take from that basin goes home, to the people who opened that door. The ore goes to their forges, and the grain to their tables. I'll never eat a crumb of it, and that suits me. I keep that reason under the spite, where the League can't get at it.

You've held a good deal of that basin for the people behind that door. The League has noticed. So have I, and so has the sergeant.

Take the Defilers' thanks. Mine is the one you'll have to ask me for twice.
