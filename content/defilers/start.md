Deathmaster Dwire, of the Defilers. The living find the title alarming. I have never had a reason to change it.

The Defilers hold Arathi Basin for the Horde, or will, once the League stops arguing the point. Everything that basin grows or digs or cuts, the Horde has a use for. The League has a use for it too. That's the quarrel, and I mean to win it.

The name Defilers? We chose it. The League would have found us a worse one, and I prefer to be insulted accurately.

There's work in the basin for anyone of the Horde. Go and spoil a human's harvest, then tell me about it.
