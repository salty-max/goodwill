The League's soldiers shout at us across the basin. Most of it is the usual: rot, curses, the Light. Every so often one of them says something worth the hearing.

At the farm last week, a young soldier of Stromgarde shouted that the field I was holding had been his grandfather's. He shouted it twice, as if I might not have understood, and then he came at me with it.

I held the field. That's what I'm for. But the thought of the old man stayed with me afterward, all through the night, and that is not what I'm for at all.

So I gave the matter some thought, the way a Forsaken does when the nights are long. The humans of Stromgarde want their fields back. They would like Arathi to be theirs again, and the past with it, and us to lie down and stay down. They can have none of it. That's spite, and I'll keep it, grandfather or no grandfather.

The Defilers hold their fields well. Keep holding them.
