Back from the basin, and the League a little hungrier for it. Good.

Everyone in the Horde fights for something. Some talk of honour, some of the land. The Forsaken have a reason too, and I'll not dress it up for you. Spite.

When we came up out of the ground, the living met us with torches and holy water. Every morning since, we have got up and gone on existing at them. There is no satisfaction like it.

Don't take it for a small thing. Spite never runs out. It needs no food, no sleep, no letters from home. A soldier who fights for a home can lose it. Nobody has ever managed to take my spite off me, and the League has tried, at length.

Keep at it.
