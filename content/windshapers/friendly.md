You stayed. Then you are owed the truth of standing with the Windshapers.

The spirits gave us their gifts, instruments and constructs both, and we tend them still. We read the wind, because the wind is the last of them that still moves. And we watch the land. It is opening, the ley lines rising through it, and our word for the wounds is scars. The magisters of the High Order would sooner study a scar than ask what made it.

The Al'Aketh we do not watch. We answer them, every time. I soften that for no one.

What you do for us is not a favor. It is part of a debt, and the debt is ours.

[after: 93836] The shrine fell and its priestess ran. Whatever shielded this island from the Windlord is gone now. I read the sky every night. So far it has only looked back.
