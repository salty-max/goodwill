Look up. Higher. You see the sprites drifting about the island and you think the winds still keep us company. They only wear the winds' shape. The spirits who carried my people into Skywall, who spoke with us and held this island up, have been silent a long time.

The Windshapers have sworn to find where they went and fetch them back, whatever it costs and however far down the road goes. Walk it with us, or walk it somewhere my word does not follow.
