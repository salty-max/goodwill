Krazek came up the stairs this morning with your name, and I sent him down again. He took it badly. Listening is the only thing he has ever been praised for.

I told him I would not be needing it, about you. Of all the people who come and go in this bay, you are the one I have stopped listening for. It took me a long time to manage it, and I am still not sure I like how it feels.

That first day, I asked you for one thing, that nothing happen while you were here. I take it back. You have been the most eventful guest this bay has had in years, and every event went my way. Cause all the trouble you like.

The red sails are still out past the point, the Venture Company still digs where I can hear it, and there will always be someone on the stairs who thinks the top floor would suit them better. That is the bay, and I would not change it. It is a little easier to run with one person in it I trust without checking.
