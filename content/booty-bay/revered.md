Krazek listens for me. He keeps to the tavern and hears every rumor that comes ashore, the strange ones especially, and brings them up the stairs. So do others whose names you will never learn. Krazek has a good ear and a poor memory for faces. For his work, that is the right way round. Most of what is said in my bay reaches me before the people saying it have finished their drinks.

That is how a baron stays a baron. Those who cross me do not get to discuss it afterwards, because I hear about it first. Lonely is what visitors imagine. Mostly it is a matter of knowing what is coming up the stairs.

Your name has come up those stairs a good deal, from this bay and from the Cartel's other towns, which tell me more than they admit. I listened to all of it. I have had people watched for less than that, and rewarded for less, and I have never once been unsure which a man deserved. With you, for a while, I was.
