Come in. Touch nothing.

I collect. Troll things, mostly, from the empire that held this jungle long before anyone thought to put a port in it. Masks, idols, blades nobody can lift. The Gurubashi made beautiful things for terrible reasons, and I have never minded the reasons.

There was one piece I wanted more than all the rest. The Stone of the Tides. I first read of it in an old book of troll legends: a stone that holds a tide, and a curse on whoever carries it, who fades out of the world and back again. A goblin wizard named Gazban was said to have found it. Gazban turned out to be a ghost. So much for the curse being a story.

[after: 611] It stands on its own pedestal now, and the tide in it never stops moving. You brought it to me, and Gazban is quiet at last. Nobody else has touched it since, and nobody will.
