Booty Bay, partner. Everyone is partner here until they prove otherwise.

Have a drink. Spend some money. I ask one thing of a guest, and it is that nothing happens while they are here. Nothing at all. My bouncers are very good at seeing to it, and they get a little extra when they have to.

You are nobody to me yet, and I mean that kindly. Most people in this bay are nobody to me. The ones who matter are the ones who do something for the Steamwheedle Cartel, and for the goblin who runs its port. Do something, and we will talk again.
