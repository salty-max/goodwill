So. Word reached me that you have been doing things for the Cartel. Now you can know what this bay is for.

Booty Bay has a fleet of its own, and the fleet is mine. The Blackwater Raiders sail out of this harbor, and my fleet master keeps the lanes open for anyone who pays the Cartel. Out past the point, a fleet under red sails would like those lanes for itself, and I take that personally. So should anyone drinking my liquor.

Inland, the Venture Company is stripping the jungle for timber and ore. They are goblins too, and that has never settled whose side a goblin is on. I have never cared for a goblin who digs where I can hear him.

Keep doing things, and the bay will start to make room for you.
