Come in, come in, come in, there is room for everyone and wonder to spare!

The Darkmoon Faire has crossed the whole of this marvelous world to set up its tents in your field. Beasts from the edges of the map. A strongwoman who lifts what cannot be lifted. A man who remembers everything you have ever told him, and a fortune teller who knows what you have not told anyone.

Buy a ticket, eat something fried, lose a little money at the games and win it back at the next. Nobody leaves the Faire the way they came in. That is a promise, and Silas Darkmoon keeps his promises, most of them.
