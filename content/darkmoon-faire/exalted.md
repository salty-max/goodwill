Stay a little. The last of the crowd has gone home.

This is the part no ticket buys, and it is the best show I have. Listen. The lanterns come down one by one. Kerri lifts her own weight bench into the wagon by herself, because she likes to. Chronos counts the takings and remembers every coin. Yebb sings to the beasts until they sleep. Rinling, off duty at last, leans on a wagon wheel and does not smile at anybody, and it is the most peaceful face in the whole Faire.

Every night I tell them this is the finest show there is, and every night I mean the tents and the lights. This is the other one. The family, putting itself away, tired and quiet and pleased.

I talk too much. You will have noticed. All the step right up and the marvel and the wonder, it is how I say thank you. I have never found another way, and I have been saying it to you for a long while now. So, once, without the drums: thank you.

Now. There is a crate of tent pegs by the wagon, and nobody here stays a guest for ever.
