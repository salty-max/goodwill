Back again! Splendid. Allow me to introduce the family.

That is Chronos, who never forgets, and who will tell you so whether you ask or not. Yebb keeps the menagerie, where nothing is quite what the sign says and everything is better. Kerri Hicks will bend an iron bar for you. Rinling took a blood oath to make every guest smile, so smile at Rinling, I beg you, for Rinling's sake.

And every one of them needs something. Leather, hammered steel, little whirring gizmos, the pelts of things with teeth, and those strange cards that turn up in the world and want to be a deck. A fair is a hungry creature. It eats what the world makes, and turns it into tickets, and the tickets into prizes, and the prizes into stories you will tell your children.

You have fed it well. The family has noticed.
