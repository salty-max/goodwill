Has Sayge read your fortune yet? Of course she has. Sayge reads everybody's.

Sayge says a fortune is only what you were going to do anyway, said out loud. I think she is right. I also think it is the most frightening thing I have ever heard said in a tent, and I have heard a great many things said in tents. Her tent is the quietest on the grounds. People go in laughing and come out thinking, and the next night some of them bring a better question.

She has never read mine. All my years on the road, and I have not answered a single one of her questions. A showman should not know how the show ends. He might start playing to the ending instead of to the crowd.

I chose the road. Nobody made me. I could have had a house and a garden and a sensible trade, and I chose a wagon and a painted sign. Every morning I choose it again. Some mornings it rains on the wagon, and I choose it anyway. Those are the mornings that count.
