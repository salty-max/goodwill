Ha! You killed many Gelkis. Their scouts spit your name now, the same spit they keep for us. Good!

You are not a centaur, but you fight a little like one. Do you laugh while you fight? You should. A warrior who laughs shows the enemy that the enemy is small, and the enemy believes it, and then he is small. That is how the Magram fight. The Gelkis sing to stones. We laugh, and we win.

The Gelkis keep crystals in a cave and call them the tears of the mother. Tears! When they are broken, the mother does not come. She never comes. One day I will break every one of them, and the Gelkis will finally know what the Magram already know.

Now we talk, you and Warug. Bring me something to laugh about.
