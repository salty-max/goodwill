Ah! You have been picking Syndicate pockets. I can always tell.

Every one of them wears an emblem, and every emblem you lift is a small disgrace for the man who wore it. We seldom need the blade. A Syndicate man who has lost his emblem has already lost the thing that mattered, and what he faces among his own is no concern of mine. I count it the prettiest work this house has ever done.

That is the whole of our art, you see. Subtlety first. Force when subtlety fails, and it fails less often than people who prefer force believe. Anyone can open a throat. Very few can open a purse in a crowded street and close it again.

You have served this house, and the guards nod you through the gate now. That is how a welcome looks at Ravenholdt.
