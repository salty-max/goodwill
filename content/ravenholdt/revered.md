I lost something once.

A small blue bag. Its contents are my own affair, and will stay so. Thieves took it, so they were good. They could not open it, so they were not good enough. Then they found a buyer for a locked mystery, a shaman in Azshara who took it for a relic. A holy thing is whatever the customer needs it to be. Then the thieves died, as people who rob me do, and I was left with a bag I could not reach, and a key that had gone somewhere worse, into the lair of a green drake in a sunken temple.

It was the one lock I could never leave alone. I would wake in the night with my fingers already moving. I have wanted very few things in my life that way, and never one so small.

[after: 8236] The key came back with you. I opened it that same night, alone, the door locked, and what lay inside lit my hands. I stayed a long while with the light on them before I let anyone in.
