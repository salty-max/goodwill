There is nothing more I can teach you.

It is the hardest sentence in my trade, and I have never once enjoyed saying it. A master is a master only as long as there is a pupil who needs him. You do not need me. Your hands are as good as mine, some days better, and your patience has outlasted every test this house could set. That is the best thing to happen to Ravenholdt in years, and I mean it, though it cost me something to get used to.

So I will give you work instead. There is a girl in the yard, all elbows and nerve, who cannot open a junkbox to save her life. Teach her. She reminds me of someone, though I could not tell you who. Use your hands, not my words. Mine have stopped working on her, and I suspect yours will not need many.

[after: 8236] And wear what I gave you. The word has gone round this house and through the Syndicate's camps, and nobody has tested it. A pity, in a way.

I will be in the hall, listening for the click from the yard.
