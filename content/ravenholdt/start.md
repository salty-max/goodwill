Welcome, welcome, welcome! Nobody finds this house by accident, which means either we wanted you found, or my guards have grown careless, and I shall be finding out which before supper.

Ravenholdt is a house of fine hands. Thieves, assassins, people who can take a ring off a finger and leave the finger none the wiser. We have quarrels, naturally. Alterac's leftovers have named themselves the Syndicate, hold their knives by the wrong end, and keep breeding more of themselves.

You are not one of mine, and I could not say yet what you are. Show me, and do it quietly. Quiet is what I am always short of.
