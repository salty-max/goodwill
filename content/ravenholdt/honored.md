So you are one of the trade after all. I suspected as much from the way you stand.

You have been bringing Fahrad boxes, I hear. Heavy, stubborn, ugly little boxes, and he sets the young ones on them because a lock is the most honest teacher in the world. It does not care who you are, or who your father was, or how much you would like it to open. It opens when your hands are right, and not one moment before. You leave a few coins in each, I am told, so the young ones have a reason to keep trying. Encouragement, Fahrad calls it. I call it bait, and I approve. They tell me I was a poor student at it once. I do not remember it that way.

Patience and hands. Everything else we teach is decoration on those two. Most who come to this house have one. Very few have both, and the ones who do are the ones I keep in mind. I have been keeping you in mind for some time.
