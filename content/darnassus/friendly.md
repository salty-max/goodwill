Your cloth went south, across the water, into Ashenvale.

The Sentinels hold the forest there against the Horde. The orcs come for the trees. The Sentinels come for the orcs. The quarrel is young, as such things go, and already it feels old, and every season it needs more bandages than the one before.

The Sentinels do not ask for much. They are proud, and they would rather bleed than be seen to need help. So the asking falls to people like me, who live in a quiet city and send cloth to a loud forest, and who are not proud at all when it comes to the Sentinels' wounds.

Your cloth was on its way to them before you had left the terrace. I sent a note with it, saying who it came from. They will not write back. Sentinels never do. But they will know.
