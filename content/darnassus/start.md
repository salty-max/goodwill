Good evening. If you would spare me a little of your time.

My people have spent these last years building a home in a place that did not exist before. A tree, grown into the sky, with a city in its branches. You may admire it. Most who come here do. They rarely ask what it cost, or what it is still costing, and I would not burden a stranger with the account.

We are short of cloth, gravely so, and the times give us no rest in which to weave our own. If you have any to spare, Darnassus would be grateful, and my people keep their gratitude a long while.
