We did not use to feel the cold.

Since the World Tree was first planted, we did not grow old, and we did not sicken, and the winter touched us lightly. Our weavers made cloth for beauty and for nothing else. A night elf might wear the same cloak for a lifetime, and a lifetime was a long thing.

That ended in the Third War. We gave up what we were to save the world, and we would give it again. But that year we became mortal, and we have been finding out since what that asks of a body. A fever that does not break. An old elf shivering by a fire that would once have been enough. A wound that will not close on its own.

So we need cloth now as we never did. For keeping each other alive. Our weavers make bandages now, and blankets, and plain warm things that will be worn through by spring, and they are learning to be proud of them.
