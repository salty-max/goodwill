Oh! Hello! You are new. Everybody in Everlook is new for about a day, and after that they are just cold, like the rest of us.

I am Umi. I live here. A gnome in a goblin trading post, up where all the roads of Winterspring cross, with nothing outside the walls but snow and things that want to eat you. I like it. Things get built here, and traded, and argued over.

My friends are clever. Every one of them always has some marvellous scheme going, and they never let me forget it. One day I am going to have a better one. I have not decided what yet.
