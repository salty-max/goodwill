I have a new idea. A good one. It is drawn out on the bench, and it is the cleverest thing I have ever thought of, and nobody in the whole world knows about it yet except me. And now you.

The surprise is that I am showing it to you before I show my friends. I always used to rush to them first, all breathless, waiting to find out whether they would laugh. I do not want to do that any more. I want to show it to someone who will ask how it works.

People ask why a gnome lives at the top of the world. The truth is that the cold keeps everyone indoors, talking, and I love the talk, even when it is about me. Lately it has been about you, and I have stopped minding that the goblins like you so much. Some days I think they have better taste than I gave them credit for.

[after: 8798] And you can build a yeti of your own now! Go and frighten someone for me. Somebody who deserves it.

So. Tell me what you think. And if it is a silly idea, tell me that too. I trust you to.
