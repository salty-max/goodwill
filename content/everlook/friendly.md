Did you know the goblins up here talk about people in other towns? All the time!

That is how the Cartel works. There are four goblin towns, the bay, Gadgetzan, Ratchet and us. They are very far apart and they gossip like neighbours. Help one of them and the others hear about it by the next caravan. Suddenly the goblins up here are friendlier to you than they have ever been to me, and I have lived here for ages!

I do not mind. Well, a little. It is nice, though, being friends with somebody the whole town likes. Legacki asked me about you just yesterday. I said you were very nice and very brave and terribly good at keeping secrets.

Which reminds me. Can you keep a secret?
