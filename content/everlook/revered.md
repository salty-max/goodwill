When my friends come up with a scheme, everybody listens. When I come up with one, they laugh first and listen after, when they listen at all. They are not mean about it. That is almost worse. They just think it is funny that the gnome has ideas too.

The yeti was my answer. I spent a long winter making something they could not laugh at, every coil of it, every bolt in its jaw. I wanted them to stop laughing so badly that I forgot to enjoy it for a while.

I have been asking myself who I build for. The ones who laugh, or the ones who ask. Legacki says I am overthinking a yeti. Legacki has never built anything that walks. I have not worked out the answer yet. I am closer, though.

[after: 5163] You helped me, and you never laughed once. You asked how it worked. You were careful with the horns. I kept thinking about that more than about whether they screamed.
