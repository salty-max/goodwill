Promise you will not tell anyone. Promise!

I am going to build a yeti. A mechanical one, with real ice thistle fur and the biggest horns, the handsome unbroken ones, and gears inside, and it is going to walk right up to my friends and scare them half to death. Legacki here, Sprinkle out in the desert, and Quixxil in the crater. All three. And then, when they have stopped screaming, they will look at it properly and see how well it is made, and they will be impressed.

That is the important part. The screaming is just for fun. The impressed is what I want.

Is it silly? It is a little silly. Engineering usually is, at the start.

[after: 5163] And you did it! You carried my yeti to every one of them. Legacki dropped a whole tray. Sprinkle still will not talk to me. I think that means it worked.
