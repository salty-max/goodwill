The Company has a long plan, and I am the only one who has drawn it.

Dun Morogh! Up north, the dwarves have springs so cold the water hurts your teeth. More water than they know what to do with. Ice falls out of their sky, for free, all winter long! One day we pipe it, or ship it, or cut it into blocks and haul it across the sea, and Gadgetzan is never thirsty again. Nobody fights over a bucket. Nobody gets killed at a well. I can stop selling power sources to people who drop them in the sand.

Until then, I sell water, and power, and patience, and I charge for all three. The money is going somewhere, is the point. I have a drawer full of drawings for it, and one drawing of a tree that grows power sources. The Company has asked me to stop bringing that one to meetings.

I would like to live long enough to be bored by water. Imagine that. A goblin in Tanaris, bored by water.
