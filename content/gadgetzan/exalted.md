Hold out your hands.

That is a cup of water. Cold and clean, from the deep well, the good one. It is free. I have practiced saying that. It still sounds strange.

Do not make that face. I know what I said the first day, and I meant it, and I still mean it for everybody else. In Gadgetzan everything costs something. But the Company owes you, and the Company pays what it owes, and today I am the Company, and I say this one is on the house.

Whatever you did for this town, you did it for a place that charged you for every sip, and you kept doing it. You put up with me. My own engineers would tell you that is the hard part.

[after: 2781] And then there is the caliph. The Company paid the bounty for him, and I added nothing to it, because the Company's money is the Company's. This cup is mine.

Drink it slowly. It tastes better when nobody is charging you for it. If word gets out that the Chief Engineer gave water away, there will be a line around the block by noon, and I will deny everything.
