Want to know how a goblin finds water in a sea of sand? Engineering! Also, a stick.

The divino-matic rod. You walk the dunes with it, and when there is water far underneath, it knows, and you dig, and the Company has a new well. Every new hole I have dug started with that rod in my hands. I would sooner lose a hand.

Then Sergeant Bly asked to borrow it. Borrow! He took it treasure hunting among the trolls of Zul'Farrak, with a band of adventurers of the worst sort, and he never brought it back. A man who steals a goblin's gold is a thief. A man who steals a goblin's divining rod in a drought is something I am not allowed to say in front of customers.

[after: 2768] It has been finding water again since it came home. The Company has stopped asking me where the new holes come from, and I have stopped explaining.
