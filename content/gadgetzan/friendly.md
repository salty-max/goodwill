Now you are someone the Company can talk to!

The situation is this. The Wastewander nomads have taken nearly every well in this desert. Our wells. We dug them, we powered them, we sent our people out to keep them running, and the nomads killed those people and kept the water. Some would call that a dispute about business. The Company calls it theft with a body count.

Their leader is called Caliph Scorpidsting. The Company has had his face drawn on a poster and a price written under it, and the price is a good one.

Whatever you have done for the Company, the Company noticed, and it does not forget who helped. One day those wells come back to us. I intend to be standing at the first one with a wrench.
