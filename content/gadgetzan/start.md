You there! Thirsty? Of course you are. This is Tanaris.

Chief Engineer, Gadgetzan Water Company. I keep the wells pumping, the pumps powered and the price fair. Fair for the Company, I mean. Every single thing in Gadgetzan costs something, water most of all, and if you came for sympathy instead of water, there is a whole desert of it outside the gate.

The nomads out there will tell you water ought to be free. The nomads also took our wells, and killed the people who kept them.
