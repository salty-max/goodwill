When Gnomeregan fell, the gnomes came down the mountain to us with nothing at all, and in the snow.

Ironforge gave them its blankets first, before it gave them a roof, and I was one of the ones handing them out. The storerooms emptied faster that winter than any raid ever managed. We gave the wool off our own beds and the padding out of our own mail, and some of the old soldiers grumbled, and gave it anyway.

I still have the first blanket I handed out. A gnome brought it back to me a year later, washed and folded, with a note thanking me for the loan. I'd not thought of it as a loan. It's in my drawer. I take it out on the cold nights.

A good share of the cloth that crosses this table still goes to them, and nobody here says a word against it. The ones who were there that first winter see to that.
