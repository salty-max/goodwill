Steelshield. You'll have wondered.

My family carried shields, and I carried one too, till a Dark Iron hammer found my knee and decided otherwise. I can walk. I can't hold a line. So they gave me this desk, and I hated it.

Making my peace with it was slow work. A shield covers one dwarf, and covers him well, if you're good. A bale of wool, sent up to the right ridge, covers a whole post. I worked that out one night with a ledger and a bottle, and I've not been sorry since. Well. Not often. My first year, I sent a crate of wool to the wrong ridge, and a post went cold for a week. I've never forgotten which ridge. I check every crate twice now.

I still keep the old shield behind the door. Every so often I take it down and polish it, and think about the ones I stood beside, and then I put it back, and I go and count wool.
