Cloth. In Ironforge. You'd think a city of stone and steel would have no use for it. You'd be wrong, and you'd not be the first.

Every dwarf in mail wears wool under it, or the mail wears the dwarf. Every miner who comes up hurt needs binding. Every bairn born in these halls needs wrapping, same as anywhere. The mountain keeps the cold off us. It does nothing for the lads on watch outside it.

Whatever you can do without, I'll use. I'll not thank you with speeches. I'll thank you by using every scrap.
