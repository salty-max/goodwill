A lad came down off the high ridge last week. Half frozen, beard full of ice, sent down with a cough the post couldn't mend. He stood at this desk dripping on my ledger and told me the socks were the only warm thing on that ridge all winter. Every dwarf up there had a pair, he said, and he'd come to say so himself because nobody else would bother.

Those socks were wool from this very storeroom. He's a good lad. He'll make a better soldier than I did, and a warmer one.

So I did a thing I've been not doing for years. I took the old shield down from behind the door, and I gave it to him, for when he goes back up. It's a good shield. It was wasted on a wall. I'll not need it again. I've got this desk, and this desk has more dwarves behind it than any shield ever did.

Steel wins the day. Wool wins the winter. I learned that one slowly, and you were standing at this desk while I learned it.

Now, the lad's waiting for his socks. Pass me that crate.
