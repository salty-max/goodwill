You. Got cloth? Then you've got something the Horde needs.

Orgrimmar fights on every front there is. The Barrens, the forest to the north, the hills over the sea. Every grunt who goes out needs wrapping before the fight and binding after. Steel comes from the smiths. Cloth comes from folk like you, or it doesn't come.

Hand it over and the grunts won't know your name. Doesn't matter. They'll know they were warm, and dry, and bound up right, and that's more than most generals ever gave them.
