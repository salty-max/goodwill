You've earned a story. It's not a happy one.

After the war, when the humans won, they put us in camps. Whole clans, behind walls, with guards on the walls and nothing to do. Day after day of nothing. You'd be surprised what nothing does to an orc. We stopped fighting each other. Then we stopped talking. Then a lot of us stopped wanting anything at all. We wore what they gave us. Sacking. Their cast-offs. Years of it, and I never once chose a thing I put on.

Then the young Warchief came and broke us out, and we came across the sea to this land. The first thing I did, before I'd laid a stone, was trade for a length of cloth and make something to wear that was mine.

That's why I took this desk. Every piece that crosses it is a choice the camps didn't let us make. Sounds like a lot to hang on some wool. It is.
