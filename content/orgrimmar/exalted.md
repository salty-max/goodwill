I cut up the rag last night.

The one I wore in the camp. What was left of it. I'd kept it all these years at the bottom of a chest. Thought if I let it go, I'd forget, and if I forgot, it could happen again.

I didn't burn it. I thought about it, most of the night. Burning's what you do to a thing you're afraid of, and I'm not afraid of it any more. So I cut it into strips and sewed them into the hem of the next red cloak, on the inside, where nobody'll find them unless they unpick it, and nobody unpicks a good cloak.

Some young orc will wear the camps against the backs of her knees and never know it. That's the right place for them. Underneath. Behind. Carrying a free orc into her first fight.

I couldn't have done it with an empty storeroom. You don't cut up your last scrap of anything. The storeroom's full because strangers kept it full. When there's enough, you can let a thing go.

She gets the cloak tomorrow. She'll take it without thinking, and run.
