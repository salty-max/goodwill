You keep your word. Good. Get in the shade a moment.

Durotar's a hard land. The sun bites and the sand gets into everything. Nobody else wanted it. Suits me. After long enough being told where to live, I'll take a place nobody can take off me.

Your wool went up as an awning in the Valley of Trials. I went down when they raised it. The young ones were drilling with axes, hours of it. The drill master called a halt. Every last one of them went under your awning at once, dropped where they stood, and slept in a heap, axes and all.

Shade's no glory. Never was. A young one who gets to rest learns faster. A grunt who gets out of the sun fights longer. I've known both kinds.
